## 0. THE TEST — why most "gaps" are not companies

An empty space is not a gap. Two kinds of emptiness, and only one is a business:

| Kind | Definition | Fate |
|---|---|---|
| **Temporarily empty** | Nobody got there yet | Absorbed into an incumbent's release note inside ~two quarters |
| **Structurally unfillable** | The party best positioned to build it is *conflicted from building it* | Survives as an independent company |

Every candidate below carries a **Barred** answer: *who is structurally prevented from filling this, and why?* A candidate with no answer there is a feature waiting to be cloned.

Archetypes used as the search frame: **neutrality** · **adversarial to the generator** · **liability not capability** · **custody**.

**Axes.** Fit = <the selection filter that applies, or "market timing — no filter"> · Open = uncrowded, 5 = wide open · Barred = strength of structural conflict · TTP = time to credible progress before the deadline · FMF = founder-market fit *already evidenced on disk* · **Reach = are the first 10–20 users nameable humans reachable today**.
